What is answer engine optimization

Answer engine optimization is the practice of structuring a page so that an AI system can retrieve it, extract a direct answer from it and cite it by name.

How engines pick sources

An engine turns the question into several searches, retrieves candidate pages, splits them into passages and ranks the passages against the question before it writes anything. Pages that answer the question in the first sentences of a section tend to supply the passage. Pages whose content only appears after JavaScript runs may never reach the passage stage at all, because several crawlers read the raw server response and nothing else. That is why server rendered text matters more here than it ever did for a plain blue link ranking. A passage has to stand on its own, so a section that opens with a pronoun pointing at the previous section is harder to quote cleanly than one that restates its subject. Short sections with one claim each are easier to lift than long essays that bury the claim.

What to change first

Open every section with the answer, name the subject in full, put the figures in sentences rather than images, and keep the date of the last review visible in both the markup and the text. Add structured data that states what the page is, who wrote it and when it changed. Check robots.txt for rules that stop search and user fetch crawlers from reading the page. Then re measure with the same prompts a month later so the change can be compared against a baseline rather than a feeling. Repeat the exercise for the five pages that drive the most revenue before touching the rest of the site. Keep a short change log so each edit can be matched to the measurement that followed it.
